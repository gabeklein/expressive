`expressive env` - `sync` writes `env.d.ts` from the `.env` files' key names and comments, `check` fails on unset keys or stale types, `list` shows each key's state and `file:line` without values, `read` pipes values, `write` sets `KEY=value` pairs or one from stdin, `unset` removes keys with their doc comments.
